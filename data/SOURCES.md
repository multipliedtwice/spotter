# Source assets

- `gazetteer.csv`: US Census 2026 National Places Gazetteer. Representative city points; never station points. Puerto Rico excluded. Geographic-name suffixes removed for city lookup.
  Override: Boston MA and San Francisco CA. Census points (42.332735,-71.013513 and 37.727239,-123.032229) sit in water outside cartographic boundary. Replaced with Census Geocoder city hall address matches (Public_AR_Current, checked 2026-11-01): 1 CITY HALL AVE Boston -> 42.357793,-71.059196; 1 DR CARLTON P GOODLETT PL San Francisco -> 37.778536,-122.41831.
  Override: 19 more coastal places whose Census points sit in water outside cartographic boundary use USGS GNIS Populated Place primary points (DomesticNames state text files, downloaded 2026-11-01): Chignik AK 1400269, Klawock AK 1423100, Valdez AK 1412465, Coronado CA 1660513, Manhattan Beach CA 1660985, Monterey CA 1659762, Santa Monica CA 1652792, South San Francisco CA 277618, Niantic CT 209276, Stonington CT 211209, Tokeneke CT 212575, Atlantic Beach FL 277982, Dunedin FL 281860, Neptune Beach FL 287587, Yankeetown FL 293564, Portland ME 573692, Grand Marais MI 627096, Keansburg NJ 882484, Ocracoke NC 1021718. Each point checked inside boundary and within 72 m of OSRM road. Full scan after override: no gazetteer point outside boundary.
  GNIS source: https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/GeographicNames/DomesticNames/
  Source: https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/maps-data/data/gazetteer/2026_Gazetteer/2026_Gaz_place_national.zip
- `us_boundary.geojson`: US Census 2025 state cartographic boundaries, 1:500,000 KML converted to GeoJSON. 50 states + DC. Territory features excluded. Generalized boundary; near-border/coast precision limited.
  Source: https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2025/kml/cb_2025_us_state_500k.zip
- `station_sample.csv`: supplied assessment rows, selected across US states, chains, exit/street/intersection address forms. Source prices unchanged.
- `station_evidence.csv`: selected source-backed station evidence. Each row links a source group to a point and retained evidence. Identity-only matches label CSV road details unverified. Prices always from supplied CSV.
- `station_unresolved.csv`: source groups without accepted coordinates. Station ID, name, address, city/state; reusable resolver sample. All original price groups retained in database.
- `pilot_unresolved.csv`: remaining unaccepted entries from the earlier Pilot candidate set. Provider-specific list, not all unresolved Pilot groups. Retained coordinates alone do not establish acceptance.
- `source_loves_locations.json`: public operator locator metadata, restricted to store number, coordinates, address, city/state, highway/exit, store type. Operator prices and contacts excluded. Source fields support independent identity/location matching; never replace supplied CSV prices.
  Source: https://www.loves.com/api/fetch_stores
  Sample corroboration: https://www.loves.com/locations/il/atkinson/loves-travel-stop-atkinson-766
- `approved_operator_locations.json`: user-approved retention of ten existing Love's source groups. Exact group fields, operator number, unchanged point, published highway/exit and source URL. Full original source description retained as containing unverified additional road or directional detail. Import rechecks approval and raw operator record; no general override for other stations.
- `approved_named_plazas.json`: user-approved Love's #176 Matfield Green source group and exact published Cassoday postal-city plaza record. Recheck source group, operator facts and I-35 mile 97; no general city alias.
- `osm_evidence.csv`, `osm_unresolved.csv`: accepted OSM-selected subset and remaining source groups by current selection. Unresolved reasons distinguish accepted using another source from unresolved across all sources; they do not claim a new OSM search. Fuel geometry and supporting identity/road context retained. Source: https://download.geofabrik.de/north-america/us.html
- `loves_evidence.csv`, `loves_unresolved.csv`: accepted Love's-selected subset and other Love's source groups by current selection. Unresolved reasons distinguish accepted using another source from unresolved across all sources; they do not claim a new operator search.
- `station_evidence.csv`: merged accepted evidence. Exact validated primary-operator points take precedence over weaker prior evidence; invalid prior points may be replaced or removed. Source prices stay from assessment CSV.
- Operator location license unknown. Store identity and location only; no prices.
- `source_directory_locations.json`: 1,052 selected public FindFuelStops `GasStation` JSON-LD records. Canonical detail URL, name, address, city/state, coordinates and country when published. Candidates only; no prices or contacts. Resolver requires independent identity and street or typed highway/marker proof. Source: https://www.findfuelstops.com/
- `source_pilot_locations.json`: selected Pilot network location-page facts. Per record: explicit store number/name, street address, published highway description, city/state, coordinates, canonical source URL. Renamed network brands remain raw source facts; no automatic Pilot/One9 alias. No prices or contact fields. Source: https://locations.pilotflyingj.com/us
- `source_kwiktrip_locations.json`: public Kwik Trip/Kwik Star store-list facts. Per record: explicit store number/name, address, city/state, coordinates, published gas/diesel flags, source URL. Non-fueling sites remain in source asset; resolver excludes them. No prices or contact fields. Source: https://www.kwiktrip.com/Maps-Downloads/Store-List
- `source_yesway_locations.json`: 426 selected native Yesway/Allsup's locator records. Published `store` label supplies store number; `acf.internal_store_code` is metadata, not a number alias. Retain selected address, point, status, amenities and fuel-card fields; missing fields stay absent. Candidates, not accepted-point count. No fuel-grade inference, prices, or contacts. Source: https://yesway.com/wp-content/themes/yesway/locations/locations.json ; locator: https://yesway.com/locations/
- `source_maverik_quiktrip_locations.json`: 289 selected Maverik/QuikTrip detail-page facts. Per record: explicit published store number/name, street address, city/state, coordinates, canonical source URL. Maverik point from public GeoCoordinates microdata; QuikTrip point from public Yext credential subject. Missing highway description stored empty. No prices or contact fields. Sources: https://locations.maverik.com/ , https://locations.quiktrip.com/
  `scoped_records`: 12 native Maverik entity facts; canonical URL, numbered entity name, US address and coordinates share one GasStation scope. QuikTrip selection requires an exact-URL credential subject.
- Operator source assets are candidates, not accepted station crosswalks. Import requires per-station identity/location checks. Published coordinates do not prove current opening status, fuel grade, or physical road access.
- `source_circlek_locations.json`: selected public Circle K API location facts for exact supplied station identifiers. Per record: raw ID, brand fields, coordinates, street/city/state/postal code/country, canonical record URL. Prices and contacts excluded. Missing or changed IDs stay unresolved; no historic-ID aliases. Source: https://api.circlek.com/us/ngrp-store-locator/v1/stations/{id} ; publisher locator: https://www.circlek.com/store-locator . Display name derived from published brand; never invents a published numbered name.
- `source_operator_roads.json`: selected OpenStreetMap road ways near fixed official operator points in Dawson GA and Hammond LA. Raw way ID, canonical URL, tags, node IDs and complete published geometry. Road evidence confirms named routes only; never supplies station coordinates. Every supplied route required within one mile of the operator point, with nearest road point inside source state. Source: https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter . OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright .
- `source_opis_locations.json`: 29 location records from the public OPIS Truckstop Spread Report sample. ID, name, street, address2, city/state/country, coordinates, source URL only; sample prices and contacts excluded. PDF rendered and checked against extracted fields. Matching requires the supplied OPIS ID, consistent name/reference, exact full address2, city/state, and state boundary. Catalog rows are candidates, not nationwide coverage. Source: https://info.opisnet.com/hubfs/Product%20Campaigns/Truckstop%20Spread%20Report/OPIS%20Truckstop%20Spread%20Report%20Sample.pdf
- `source_ta_locations.json`: selected TA, TA Express, Petro location-page JSON-LD. Published name, number, street, city/state, point, description, URL only. No prices or contacts. Source: https://www.ta-petro.com/location/
- `source_roadranger_locations.json`: paired Road Ranger billing and locator facts.
